Operated by the US Army and US Navy throughout WWII. Using 15 rotors across three functional banks with dynamic electrical stepping, SIGABA was never broken by Axis cryptanalysts.

Cracked commercial and military Enigma in the early 1930s using group theory permutations, the Cyclometer, and perforated sheets, giving Britain the head start needed to crack wartime traffic.
